Houses & Hostels Disclaimer

Important — please read before contacting any agent.

  1. No online payment for houses or hostels is processed on SunGarland. Paystack and card checkout do not apply to this section.
  2. Do not pay any deposit, rent, or “reservation fee” until you have seen the property in person.
  3. Confirm the agent is the person in charge (and is government-registered). Approved agent status on this site helps but does not replace your own checks.
  4. Meet in a safe place when possible. Prefer daylight viewings. Do not share OTPs or send money to unknown mobile numbers in advance.
  5. SunGarland lists agent contacts for convenience only. Rental agreements are between you and the agent/landlord.
  6. If something looks like a scam, use Report immediately and stop communication.

By using Houses & Hostels you acknowledge these rules.
